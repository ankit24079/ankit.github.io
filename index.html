About

Building dependable autonomy systems for drones and mobile robots.

I am a Master's student in Robotics at the University of Minnesota, specializing in perception, SLAM, and navigation. My work combines computer vision, multi-sensor fusion, and embedded deployment for robotic platforms that need to operate in real time.

Twin Cities, Minnesota
